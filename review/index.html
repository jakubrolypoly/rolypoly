How to connect your Grok Bot
  1. Ask your Grok Bot to create a routine with a webhook trigger that treats incoming payloads as review notes.
  2. Open the routine panel and copy the webhook URL and the sender key.
  3. Click Settings on this page and paste them in.
  4. Press Send test ping. Your bot should confirm it got the ping.
After that, Send to my bot delivers your notes straight to your bot. Copy all notes still works if you prefer to paste them into a chat.
